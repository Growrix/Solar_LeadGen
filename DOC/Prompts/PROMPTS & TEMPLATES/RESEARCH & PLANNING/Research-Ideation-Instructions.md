# Research & Ideation Instruction

## Purpose
Guide the user (or AI) to research and brainstorm a feature or idea in a way that produces clear, actionable, and user-centric outcomes for further planning and development.

---

## Workflow Steps

### 1. Define the Problem or Opportunity
- Clearly state the problem, goal, or opportunity you want to address.
- Specify the target users or stakeholders.

### 2. Research User Needs & Pain Points
- Identify who the users are and what their main needs, goals, and frustrations are.
- Gather insights from:
  - User interviews or surveys
  - Online forums, reviews, or feedback
  - Analytics or usage data

### 3. List Features, Flows, and Use Cases
- Brainstorm all possible features, user flows, and use cases that could address the problem.
- Include both core and advanced/optional ideas.
- Think about:
  - What users want to achieve
  - What actions they need to take
  - What outcomes or value they expect

### 4. Benchmark Competitors or Similar Products
- Research how other products or competitors solve similar problems.
- Note best practices, unique features, and gaps.

### 5. Brainstorm Enhancements & Automation
- Consider how AI, automation, or integrations could improve the experience.
- List any future-proofing or scalability ideas.

### 6. Output Format
- Produce a raw, comprehensive list of user stories, features, and ideas.
- Do not worry about formatting for development yet—focus on completeness and clarity.
- Example output:
  - User stories (even if rough):
    - "As a user, I want to..."
  - Feature/idea lists
  - Notes on pain points, opportunities, and enhancements

---

## Instructions
- Be exhaustive and creative—capture all ideas, even if not all will be built.
- Do not filter or prioritize at this stage.
- Save the output for use in the Initial Plan Finalization phase.
